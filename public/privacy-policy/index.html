Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the “Pedometer walking step Counter” app (the “App”), developed and published by Health and Fitness Apps Group (the “Developer”), accesses, collects, uses, processes, and shares information.

The App is supported by advertising.

IMPORTANT: The App is not a medical device. It is not intended for medical diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or monitoring of any health condition.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App uses third-party services for advertising, advertising mediation, analytics, diagnostics, and related technical functionality.

These third-party services may collect, receive, and process certain user and device information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The types of information that may be processed are described below.

1.1 Physical Activity and Step Data

The App uses Android physical activity capabilities, including the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission and supported device step or motion sensors, to provide its core step-counting functionality.

Physical activity data is used to:

  • calculate and display step counts;
  • calculate related activity statistics, such as distance, calories, and activity duration;
  • display daily progress, goals, statistics, and step history;
  • provide achievements and other step-related functionality.

Step-count information and related physical activity data used for these core functions are processed and stored locally on the user's device.

The App does not upload the user's individual step history or raw physical activity sensor data to servers operated by the Developer.

The App does not use physical activity data to provide medical diagnosis, medical treatment, or medical advice.
